        Shubha Muhurtham

        Days with a good tithi, nakshatra and weekday, outside Aadi, Purattasi and Margazhi and not a Kari naal, with time windows that avoid Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika Kalam and Vishti karana and have no planet in the 8th house from the lagna. Personal factors such as tarabala and chandrabala aren't considered, and panchangams differ, so confirm with your family astrologer.

        Free Vedic calendar app: daily Panchangam with tithi, nakshatra, Rahu Kalam and hora, Tamil calendar dates, Hindu festival dates and puja times, Shubha Muhurtham days with lagna, and South Indian birth charts with Vimshottari dasha. Made for places where printed panchangams aren't available: everything is calculated for your own location, in local time.

        Your data stays with you. No sign-up and no cookies, and your birth date and time never leave your device. The site counts anonymous page views only. Places not in the built-in list are looked up on OpenStreetMap.

        Calculations run on your device using the Swiss Ephemeris (AGPL-3.0). Place names from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0) and OpenStreetMap. Source code.
