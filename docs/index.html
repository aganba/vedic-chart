        Rules used

        Events, by the Moon's nakshatra

        • Vehicles and travel: movable nakshatras (Punarvasu, Swati, Shravana, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha).
        • Day-to-day matters: mixed nakshatras (Krittika, Vishakha).
        • Learning music and arts, and auspicious events: soft nakshatras (Mrigashira, Chitra, Anuradha, Revati).
        • Sports, new projects and travel: light nakshatras (Ashwini, Pushya, Hasta, Abhijit).
        • Sharp (Ardra, Ashlesha, Jyeshtha, Mula) and dreadful (Bharani, Magha, Purva Phalguni, Purva Ashadha, Purva Bhadrapada) nakshatras are avoided.

        Every time

        • Tithi: not Navami or Chaturdashi.
        • Panchanga yoga: not Atiganda, Shula, Ganda, Vyaghata, Vyatipata, Parigha or Vaidhriti.
        • Karana: not Vishti, Shakuni or Chatushpada.
        • Lagna Thyajya: the lagna isn't in the first 3° of Mesha, Vrishabha, Kanya or Dhanu, the last 3° of Karka, Vrischika, Makara or Meena, or 13°30′–16°30′ of Mithuna, Simha, Tula or Kumbha.
        • Panchaka: the numbers of the tithi (1–30), weekday (Sunday = 1), nakshatra (Ashwini = 1) and lagna (Mesha = 1) add up to a remainder of 0, 3, 5 or 7 when divided by 9. The weekday runs from sunrise to sunrise.
        • Surya Sankramana: not within 16 ghatis (6 hours 24 minutes) of the Sun entering a sign.
        • Not in Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda or Gulika Kalam, and not on an eclipse day.
        • At least 20 minutes long.
        • Between an hour before sunrise and two hours after sunset. This is a practical limit for these events, not one of Niranjan Babu's rules.
        • Vijayadashami is traditionally auspicious for every beginning, so it is always listed, for all events and all day, even when the rules above don't pick it.

        Rules used

        Every day

        • Weekday: Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday or Friday; never Tuesday or Saturday.
        • Tamil month: not Aadi, Purattasi or Margazhi, and not the first day of any month.
        • Not a Kari naal: Chithirai 6, 15; Vaikasi 7, 16, 17; Aani 1, 6; Aadi 2, 10, 20; Avani 2, 9, 28; Purattasi 16, 29; Aippasi 6, 20; Karthigai 1, 10, 17; Margazhi 6, 9, 11; Thai 1, 2, 3, 11, 17; Maasi 15, 16, 17; Panguni 6, 15, 19.
        • Yogam of the day (from the weekday and the nakshatra at sunrise): Amirtha or Siddha, not Marana or Prabalarishta.
        • Not an eclipse day (a solar or lunar eclipse anywhere that day).

        Every time

        • Tithi: Dwitiya, Tritiya, Chaturthi, Panchami, Shashthi, Saptami, Dashami, Ekadashi, Dwadashi or Trayodashi of either paksha, or Valarpirai Chaturdashi. Never Pratipada, Ashtami, Navami, Purnima, Amavasya or Theipirai Chaturdashi.
        • Nakshatra: Ashwini, Rohini, Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Pushya, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Chitra, Swati, Anuradha, Mula, Uttara Ashadha, Shravana, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha, Uttara Bhadrapada or Revati.
        • Between an hour before sunrise (not before 5 am) and two hours after sunset.
        • Outside Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda and Gulika Kalam, and not in Vishti (Bhadra) karana.
        • No planet, the Moon, Rahu and Ketu included, in the 8th house from the lagna.
        • The lagna isn't in the first or last degree of its sign.
        • At least 20 minutes long.

        Not applied

        • Panchanga yoga (Vyatipata, Vaidhriti and the like), moudhyam (Jupiter or Venus combust), and Dr. B. V. Raman's marriage rules (an empty 7th house, no malefic in the lagna).
        • Personal factors: tarabala, chandrabala and chandrashtamam.
