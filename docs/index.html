        Shubha Muhurtham

        Days and times in the style of Tamil calendars, for marriage and other auspicious beginnings, calculated for the place you entered. Panchangams differ, and personal factors such as tarabala, chandrabala and chandrashtamam aren't considered, so confirm with your family astrologer.

        Rules used

        The day

        • Weekday: not Tuesday or Saturday.
        • Tithi: not Pratipada, Ashtami, Navami, Purnima, Amavasya or Theipirai Chaturdashi.
        • Nakshatra: Ashwini, Rohini, Mrigashira, Punarvasu, Pushya, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Chitra, Swati, Anuradha, Mula, Uttara Ashadha, Shravana, Dhanishta, Shatabhisha, Uttara Bhadrapada or Revati.
        • Tamil month: not Aadi, Purattasi or Margazhi, and not the first day of a month.
        • Not a Kari naal.
        • Yogam (from the weekday and the nakshatra at sunrise): Amirtha or Siddha, not Marana or Prabalarishta.
        • Not an eclipse day, and neither Jupiter nor Venus combust (moudhyam).

        The time

        • Between an hour before sunrise (not before 5 am) and sunset.
        • Outside Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika Kalam and Vishti karana.
        • Panchanga yoga: not Vyatipata or Vaidhriti, not the first half of Parigha, and not the first 3 ghatis (72 minutes) of Vishkambha or Vajra, 5 of Shula, 6 of Ganda or Atiganda, or 9 of Vyaghata.
        • No planet in the 7th or 8th house from the lagna.
        • The lagna isn't in the first or last degree of its sign.
        • At least 20 minutes long.

        Sources

        • Weekday, tithi, nakshatra, month, Kari naal and time of day follow Tamil calendar practice; Rahu Kalam, Yamaganda, Gulika Kalam, Vishti karana, the 8th house, eclipses, moudhyam and lagna sandhi follow general panchangam practice. The panchanga yoga rule follows classical muhurtha texts such as Muhurta Chintamani; many Tamil calendars don't apply it, so a few of their days aren't listed here.
        • The empty 7th house and the rules for the extra education days: B. V. Raman, Muhurtha or Electional Astrology, chapters 9 and 11 (chapter 9 summary, chapter 11 summary)
        • Amirtha, Siddha, Marana and Prabalarishta yogam by weekday and nakshatra: Ungal Vazhkkai Vazhikatti
